METHODOLOGY & LIMITATIONS

How to read these findings

OVERLAPPING RECORDS

Jio and Airtel contain overlapping customer records/statuses. Do not treat their totals or repeated patterns as independent company evidence or unique people.

NETFLIX LABELS

Dataset provenance, target meaning, and behavior feature timing are unverified. Ten daily-watch values exceed 24 hours, maximum 98.42; no unusual values were removed or changed.

AMAZON PRIME

Prime Renewal Status is only Manual/Auto-renew and is not churn. The generic educational proxy dataset/model is separate and is not used in these Prime charts or counts.

DIFFERING DATASETS

Dataset sizes, columns, target definitions, and provenance differ. Cross-company rates are shown separately and should not be interpreted as a fair head-to-head comparison.

DATA HANDLING

Charts use observed source records. Category-rate comparisons use explicit Yes/No responses; missing or other categories are not relabeled as either value. Negative Airtel charges and Netflix watch-time outliers are retained.

Primary sources: Jio_Customer_Data.csv, Main_churn_data_for_tableau.csv, netflix_customer_churn.csv, and amazon_prime_users.csv. The generic Amazon proxy training/testing files are excluded from primary-dataset KPIs and charts.
