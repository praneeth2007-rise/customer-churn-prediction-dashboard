ABOUT THE PROJECT

Customer Churn Prediction and Analytics Dashboard

This academic project uses machine learning and data analytics to study customer churn and estimate churn risk from available customer datasets. It brings together exploratory insights, saved classification models, and interactive company dashboards while making dataset scope and limitations visible.

THE PROBLEM

Why study customer churn?

01

What churn means

Customer churn is when a customer stops using or renewing a product or service during a defined period. A reliable churn label depends on a clear business rule and observation window.

02

Why retention matters

Retaining customers can support continuity and long-term relationships. Organizations study customer behavior to understand where experience, usage, or service patterns differ.

03

Where analytics helps

Predictive analytics can estimate which customers resemble previously labeled churn cases, helping teams investigate risk signals and plan measured retention work.

CLASSIFICATION APPROACHES

Models used by dataset

JIO

Random Forest

Combines votes from many decision trees. The ensemble can represent non-linear feature relationships and classifies the Jio churn task.

AIRTEL

Logistic Regression

Combines weighted input features through a logistic function to estimate class probability. It is a common, relatively simple classification approach used for Airtel.

NETFLIX

Random Forest

Aggregates decision-tree votes to classify the dataset's churn label. Its result needs caution because provenance and target meaning are unverified.

AMAZON GENERIC PROXY

Random Forest

Uses the repaired pipeline trained on separate generic churn data. It is educational proxy work, not an Amazon Prime-specific model.

DATASETS

Four different data scopes

JIO

Jio customer dataset

Jio_Customer_Data.csv has customer, service, revenue, and Customer_Status fields. Joined is separate from Stayed and Churned analytics.

AIRTEL

Airtel customer dataset

Main_churn_data_for_tableau.csv includes customer, service, charges, revenue, and status fields. Reports identify record overlap with Jio.

NETFLIX

Netflix customer dataset

netflix_customer_churn.csv has a binary churned field and viewing/subscription features. Provenance, label definition, and behavior timing are unverified.

AMAZON PRIME

Prime user dataset

amazon_prime_users.csv supports subscription and usage insights but has no verified churn target. Manual / Auto-renew describes preference, not churn. Generic proxy data is separate.

MODEL EVALUATION

What the metrics describe

Open model comparison →
ACCURACY

Share of evaluated records classified correctly.

PRECISION

Among predicted churners, share labeled churn.

RECALL

Among labeled churners, share detected.

F1-SCORE

Harmonic mean of churn precision and recall.

CONFUSION MATRIX

Counts true/false positives and negatives to show error types.

The comparison page uses reported results. Dataset sizes, labels, provenance, and evaluation setups differ, so scores are not a like-for-like ranking.
LIMITATIONS

What to keep in mind

JIO + AIRTEL

Records and status labels overlap; they are not independent evidence.

NETFLIX

Provenance and target definition are unverified. Ten daily-watch values exceed 24 hours; maximum 98.42.

AMAZON PRIME

No verified churn target exists; renewal preference is not churn.

AMAZON PROXY

Generic proxy results are not Amazon Prime performance.

PREDICTIONS

Model outputs are estimates, not guarantees of future behavior.
